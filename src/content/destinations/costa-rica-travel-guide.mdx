## Introduction

Costa Rica is one of those rare destinations that genuinely has something for everyone. You can hike an active volcano in the morning, soak in natural thermal springs by afternoon, and zipline through the rainforest canopy the next day, all without changing hotels. The country packs an incredible range of experiences into a compact, accessible footprint. Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, a family adventure, a trip with friends, or a corporate retreat, Costa Rica adapts to what you need. And unlike destinations where you're choosing between relaxation and adventure, here you get both without compromise.

</Intro>

<Panel image="/images/costa-rica-volcano.webp" alt="Arenal Volcano framed by palm trees in Costa Rica">

La Fortuna is the gateway to Costa Rica's adventure heartland. Sitting at the base of Arenal Volcano, one of the most iconic volcanoes in Central America, the area is surrounded by dense rainforest, waterfalls, hot springs, and rivers. It's become one of the most popular destinations in the country for good reason: the concentration of things to do here is hard to beat.

The town itself is small and walkable with restaurants, shops, and local tour operators. But the real draw is what surrounds it. Arenal National Park offers guided volcano hikes ranging from easy walks to more challenging trails, all with views of the volcanic landscape and surrounding forest canopy. La Fortuna Waterfall is a 230-foot cascade you can hike down to and swim at the base. And the area's natural thermal springs, heated by volcanic activity underground, are some of the best in the world.

Getting there is straightforward. Most visitors fly into Liberia International Airport (LIR), which has direct flights from major U.S. cities. From there, it's about a two-and-a-half-hour scenic drive through the Costa Rican countryside. The landscape changes dramatically along the way. Dry tropical plains near the coast give way to rolling green hills and eventually thick rainforest as you approach the volcano.

</Panel>

<Panel image="/images/pool-in-costa-rica-resort.webp" alt="Pool in a Costa Rica resort">

La Fortuna has accommodations for every budget, but the area is especially known for its luxury eco-resorts that blend high-end amenities with the natural surroundings.

The standout is Tabacon Resort & Spa, an award-winning property set in the rainforest at the base of Arenal. Tabacon is built around natural thermal springs that are included for all hotel guests, so no day pass is needed. The resort's spa is world-renowned for its open-air treatment rooms nestled in the jungle, and it has multiple on-site restaurants ranging from casual to fine dining. Breakfast is included daily with a full spread that goes well beyond the typical hotel buffet.

For travelers looking for something more intimate, boutique jungle lodges along the rivers offer treehouse-style rooms, outdoor dining, and direct access to nature activities like river tubing and kayaking. These properties tend to feel more immersive and off-the-grid while still being comfortable.

Budget-friendly options exist too. La Fortuna town has a range of mid-tier hotels and hostels, and many of the area's best experiences (waterfall hikes, town exploration, public hot springs) are accessible regardless of where you stay.

The area also works well for larger groups. Several resorts accommodate parties of 30 to 60 with private dining, group excursion coordination, and flexible event spaces, making La Fortuna increasingly popular for retreats, incentive trips, and milestone celebrations.

### Things to Do

**Arenal Volcano Hike.** The national park offers multiple trail options through hardened lava fields and rainforest, with views of the volcano's massive cone. Guided hikes run about an hour to an hour and a half, with difficulty levels that work for most fitness levels.

**Ziplining.** Costa Rica is one of the best places in the world for canopy ziplining, and La Fortuna delivers. Go Adventure Park features seven lines spanning roughly two miles through the rainforest, with an optional 50-meter cliff rappel at the end.

**Thermal Springs.** This is what makes La Fortuna special. The volcanic activity beneath the surface heats natural springs throughout the region. Tabacon's springs are among the most famous, with multiple temperature pools winding through landscaped tropical gardens.

**Water Activities.** River rafting on the Pacuare or Balsa rivers, kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding on Lake Arenal, and waterfall swimming are all within easy reach. La Fortuna Waterfall alone is worth the trip. The hike down is steep but manageable, and swimming at the base of a 230-foot waterfall is an unforgettable experience.

**Wildlife & Nature.** Costa Rica is one of the most biodiverse countries on the planet. In the La Fortuna area you'll encounter toucans, howler monkeys, sloths, colorful frogs, and more, often without even trying.

### Who Costa Rica Is For

**Couples.** The combination of luxury resorts, natural hot springs, world-class spas, and rainforest dining makes La Fortuna one of the most romantic destinations in Central America. It's the kind of place where you can do an adventure together in the morning and have an intimate dinner in the jungle that night.

**Families.** Costa Rica is incredibly family-friendly. The activities are accessible to a wide range of ages and abilities, the wildlife is fascinating for kids, and the resorts cater to families with flexible dining and excursion options. It's adventure travel without the intimidation factor.

**Friend Groups.** This is a destination where everyone gets what they want. The adventure seekers can zipline and hike while the relaxation crew posts up at the hot springs. Everyone meets back up for dinner with stories to share. It solves the "where should we go" debate.

**Corporate & Large Groups.** La Fortuna's resort infrastructure supports groups well, with private dining, customizable excursions, and natural settings that encourage organic team building. The mix of shared adventure and downtime creates the kind of connection that conference rooms can't replicate.

**Solo Travelers.** Costa Rica is one of the safest and most welcoming countries in Central America for solo travelers. English is widely spoken, the tourism infrastructure is well-developed, and the "pura vida" culture is genuinely warm. La Fortuna's walkable town and organized tour options make it easy to explore independently.
